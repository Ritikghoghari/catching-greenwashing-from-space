## Week 2 — Scoring Layer (0-100)
### (Thesis no main contribution)

**Score = 3 cheezno combination:**

```
Score = (Forest Loss Weight × 0.4)
      + (Claim Specificity Weight × 0.4)
      + (Spatial Match Weight × 0.2)
```

- Forest Loss — post-2020 ha, normalised (% of forest use karo, total nahi)
- Claim Specificity — ClimateBERT specificity score (continuous, 0-1)
- Spatial Match — loss kya claim na region ma aave che?

**Sensitivity Analysis:**
- 5km buffer vs 10km vs 15km — ranking badlay che?
- Percentage weight vs total hectares — difference check

**Writing saath:**
- Methodology chapter start (2,000 words)
