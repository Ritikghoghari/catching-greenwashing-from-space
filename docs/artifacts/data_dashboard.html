Post-2020 Forest Loss by Company (hectares)

Total forest loss after the EUDR cutoff date (31 Dec 2020) within 10 km of each company's mills. 9 of 11 companies have at least one hard/specific no-deforestation-style claim on record; Wilmar and Genting have none.

Key finding: GAR shows the highest total loss (171,236 ha) because it has the most mills (50). But IOI has the highest per-mill intensity (4,959 ha/mill). Total hectares and per-mill intensity tell different stories — the scoring layer must account for this.

Claim Strength — Share of Hard-Language Claims

% of each company's kept environmental claims using specific, measurable language ("no deforestation", "zero", "deforestation-free") vs. vague/aspirational wording. Feeds the mismatch score's claim-strength half.

Forest Loss per Mill (ha/mill)

Average loss per mill — the most direct intensity measure. IOI ranks #1 here despite having fewer total mills than GAR or Wilmar.

Claim vs. Reality — Where the Mismatch Score Comes From

X-axis: claim strength (% hard-language claims). Y-axis: forest-loss score (normalized ha/mill, 0-100). Bubble size = number of mills. Top-right = strong claims + high measured loss = biggest gap.

IOI and KLK sit top-right — strong/hard claims paired with high measured forest-loss intensity, driving their #1 and #2 mismatch scores. Wilmar and Genting made no hard claims at all (0% claim strength), so despite real forest loss their mismatch score is low — there's no strong claim to contradict.

Forest Loss — Before and After the EUDR Cutoff

Orange = total forest lost 2001-2020. Red = loss specifically after 2020 (EUDR-relevant window). Stacked to show proportion.

Year-by-Year Loss — Wilmar Sabahmas Mill

Post-2020 clearing at one mill. Shows that loss continued every year after the NDPE commitment — not a one-off event.

Number of Mills per Company

Mill count drives total loss. Companies with more mills cover more land — this is why per-mill and % metrics matter alongside absolute hectares.
